📊 Project

1. Royal Choice Supermarket Management Analysis 

Project Overview

The Royal Choice Supermarket Management System is an Excel-based business management solution developed to organize and analyze supermarket sales, inventory, customer transactions, payments, costs, and profit.

The system was designed to replace poorly organized manual records with a structured system that makes daily transactions easier to record, monitor, and analyze.

Problem Statement

Supermarkets handle a large number of daily transactions involving different products, customers, quantities, prices, payment methods, costs, and profits.

When these records are managed manually, businesses may experience difficulties such as:

- Inaccurate sales records
- Difficulty tracking transactions
- Errors in calculating sales and profit
- Difficulty monitoring inventory
- Poor payment records
- Difficulty retrieving previous transactions
- Limited visibility into business performance

Problem Solving

The system addresses these challenges by providing a structured and automated approach to supermarket management.

Sales Management

A Sales Database records important transaction information, including:

- Receipt Number
- Customer ID
- Date
- Transaction ID
- Product Name
- Product ID
- Category
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Total Sales
- Unit Cost
- Total Cost
- Profit
- Payment Method

Inventory Management

Product information and stock levels are organized to make it easier to monitor available products and identify inventory-related issues.

Sales and Profit Calculation

Excel formulas are used to calculate:

- Total Sales
- Total Cost
- Profit

This reduces the need for repeated manual calculations.

Payment Tracking

The system records different payment methods, including:

- Cash
- Transfer
- POS

This makes payment records easier to monitor and analyze.

Receipt and Transaction Tracking

Each transaction is assigned a unique Receipt Number and Transaction ID, making individual sales easier to identify and retrieve.

Customer Management

Customer IDs are connected to sales transactions, allowing customer purchases to be properly associated with their transactions.

Data Accuracy

Structured tables, formulas, and data validation help reduce incorrect data entry and maintain consistency in the records.
